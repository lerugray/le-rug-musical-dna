## What Changed


### Density
**Densest album:** Don't Wait at 11.0 n/s — **Sparsest album:** Defenestrator at 2.8 n/s. That's a 8.2 n/s spread across the catalog, a significant range that suggests real stylistic evolution.




### Tempo
**Fastest album:** Don't Wait at 208 BPM — **Slowest album:** Defenestrator at 155 BPM. A 53 BPM spread — enough to feel like a different energy level.




### Harmonic Ambiguity Trend
Harmonic ambiguity rate **trends upward** over the catalog — from 58.0% (Albert Liberto — SoundCloud Instrumentals) to 52.2% (True Crime). More of the later chord events register as root+5th with no detectable third — consistent with a heavier, more distorted sound.
