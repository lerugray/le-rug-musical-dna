## Context

Butter the Children's debut — predates True Crime by two years. Ray wrote all the material and played both guitar and drums, with Inna on vocals and Max Kagan on bass. Different lineup from True Crime (which had J Boxer on bass and Jordyn Blakely on drums, produced by Jesse Harris). Scott Andrews produced and engineered.

Track titles reference Seinfeld episodes (Rochelle Rochelle, Prognosis Negative) — a running joke.
