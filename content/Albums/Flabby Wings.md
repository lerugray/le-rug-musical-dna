# Flabby Wings (Ray Weiss solo)

**18 tracks** | **64.8 min** | **203 BPM avg** | **8.9 n/s**

A collection of solo instrumental recordings. Ray playing everything — guitar, bass, drums. These overlap with the SoundCloud instrumentals (albert-liberto account) and represent the most concentrated period of solo writing.

## Key Findings

**The densest album-length collection.** At 8.9 n/s across 18 tracks and over an hour, Flabby Wings is denser on average than any Le Rug album. Only ABH (9.1) and True Crime (9.2) match it, and those are shorter.

**Includes the densest track in the catalog.** Indefinite Diagnosis at 17.2 n/s remains the single densest recording across all material — tied with Bleenex's "Gloss" for the top spot.

**The Movement Suite** — Movements 1-4 form a continuous 23-minute piece:
- Movement 1: 14.7 n/s (3.0m) — ultra-dense opener
- Movement 2: 11.1 n/s (4.0m) — still very dense
- Movement 3: 11.2 n/s (4.0m) — sustained intensity
- Movement 4: 4.5 n/s (12.0m) — the 12-minute wind-down

This is the longest sustained composition — 23 minutes total, moving from frenetic density to a long, spacious conclusion. Nothing else in the catalog attempts this kind of arc.

**C/G-centered** — C5 (685) and G5 (577) dominate, with G major triad (419) unusually prominent. The presence of full G major triads at this level is notable — these instrumentals commit to major tonality more than the vocal material typically does.
