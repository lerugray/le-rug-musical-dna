# Defenestrator

**5 tracks** | **40.3 min** | **168 BPM avg** | **3.6 n/s**

Ray's solo noise/hardware project. Instruments included a Make Noise Strega and other hardware synths — no guitar-based songwriting, just texture and sound design.

## Key Findings

**The slowest, sparsest project in the catalog.** At 3.6 n/s and 168 BPM, Defenestrator is the polar opposite of Le Rug. Only Sticky Buns (3.5 n/s) is comparably sparse, and even that has more conventional song structure.

**The longest tracks by far.** Predator Vision runs 15.0 minutes — the single longest recording in the entire catalog. Catatonic Schizophrenic is 9.4 minutes. These are durational pieces, not songs.

**Two distinct modes:**
- **Ambient/drone** — Aquatic Amphetamines (1.0 n/s, 118 BPM) and Predator Vision (2.4 n/s, 160 BPM) are near-ambient. Aquatic Amphetamines at 1.0 n/s is the sparsest thing Ray has ever recorded.
- **Aggressive noise** — MES Ascends to Heaven (8.2 n/s, 211 BPM) flips completely into dense, fast territory. The title references Mark E. Smith (The Fall) — fitting for the most intense Defenestrator track.

**C/D power chord center** — C5 (365) dominates massively, with D5 (134) and A#5 (84) behind. The A# prominence is unusual — this is one of the few places in Ray's catalog where A# plays a significant role.

## Notes

Tracks were recovered from two separate sources. "Bozo" may have been a working title or alternate release grouping.

This project represents the furthest Ray ever got from song-based music. The hardware-driven approach strips away everything that defines the Le Rug sound — no power chords as foundation, no octave doubling, no short-burst songwriting. What remains is texture, duration, and a willingness to sit in a sound.
